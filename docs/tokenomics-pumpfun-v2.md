# Climboria ($CLIMB) Tokenomics — Pump.fun Launch Model

**Status:** Working policy draft — token not launched, supply/mint not verified, and no rewards are guaranteed.  
**Launch route:** Standard Pump.fun launch on Solana first. Base is a later phase.

## Core approach

Climboria will prioritize the playable game, community growth, giveaways, and transparent promotion rather than running a dedicated pre-launch airdrop campaign.

The former November 1 airdrop campaign target is removed from the tokenomics plan. Do not advertise an airdrop campaign or guaranteed token allocation unless a new, separately approved and funded plan is published.

## Supply and launch

- **Proposed supply target:** 1,000,000,000 $CLIMB, subject to confirmation against Pump.fun's current launch configuration and the actual mint.
- **Launch method:** Standard Pump.fun bonding-curve launch; graduation/migration follows the platform's current mechanics if the threshold is reached.
- **Liquidity:** Do not treat a separate 15% liquidity allocation as a project-controlled reserve. Pump.fun controls its launch curve and graduation mechanism. No promise that the founder will buy tokens or contribute personal liquidity capital.
- **Ticker and official mint:** $CLIMB is a candidate ticker until availability and the official mint are verified. No official token mint address has been published.
- **Base:** Later phase only, after supply accounting and the cross-chain model are documented.

## Rewards and community policy

### 1. Giveaways and promotions
- Use occasional, clearly announced giveaways and community contests instead of a dedicated pre-launch airdrop campaign.
- Every giveaway must publish its rules, eligibility, closing time, prize, selection method, and how winners are contacted.
- Never imply a prize is guaranteed before it is funded and available.
- If prizes are paid in $CLIMB, distribute only tokens actually controlled by the project or purchased through an explicitly funded, disclosed budget. Do not promise a token prize before the mint and source are verified.
- No requirement to connect a wallet to a suspicious site, share a seed phrase, or pay a fee to claim a giveaway prize.

### 2. Gameplay rewards
- Start with off-chain CLIMB Points, levels, streaks, referrals, events, Forge progression, and badges where implemented.
- Points are not tokens, are not transferable, have no guaranteed monetary value, and do not guarantee future token rewards.
- Any future on-chain reward program needs a real funding source, published rules, anti-bot controls, and security review.

### 3. Creator fees
- Creator fees may support development, operations, community giveaways, and promotion only when actually received and after applicable costs.
- Revenue is variable and depends on trading activity and current Pump.fun rules. No fixed income or yield is promised.
- Do not enable a creator-fee-to-holder mode unless the project deliberately chooses to forgo the creator-wallet fee flow and confirms the current platform rules before launch.

## Budget categories (not token allocation promises)

| Category | Policy |
|---|---|
| Launch / liquidity | Pump.fun standard launch and graduation mechanics. |
| Community giveaways | Occasional funded contests and prizes; budget only when funds or tokens are actually available. |
| Promotion | Organic content, creator/community collaborations, and explicitly funded campaigns. |
| Gameplay rewards | CLIMB Points first; on-chain rewards only when funded and documented. |
| Development / operations | Actual project resources or realized revenue only. |
| Team / partnerships | No implied token reserve or vesting commitment until a genuine source and schedule are published. |
| Future Forge, badges, own DEX, app, Base | Roadmap only; no implied funding or token reserve. |

The previous percentage pie chart (20% community/airdrop, 20% gameplay, 15% liquidity, 15% treasury, 10% development, 10% team, 5% marketing, 3% partnerships, 2% security) is retired and must not be presented as reserved supply or a commitment for a standard Pump.fun launch.

## Launch readiness checks

Before announcing a launch, verify:
- current Pump.fun supply, curve, graduation/migration, fees, and creator-fee options;
- token ticker availability and actual mint;
- giveaway rules and actual prize funding;
- official website/social links and anti-scam warnings;
- whether any on-chain reward distribution is feasible and funded.

**Dates:** The previous November 1 airdrop campaign target is removed. Any future TGE window is a target only until launch readiness is confirmed. Do not announce a guaranteed date, airdrop, price, or payout.
