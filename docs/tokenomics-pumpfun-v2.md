# Climboria ($CLIMB) Tokenomics — Pump.fun Launch Model (Working Draft)

**Status:** Proposal only — not final, not deployed, and not a promise of token allocation or rewards.  
**Launch route:** Standard Pump.fun launch on Solana first. Base is a later phase.

## Why the model is changing

The earlier proposal used a 1,000,000,000 $CLIMB supply split into percentage allocations, including a separate 15% liquidity bucket. That model assumes Climboria can pre-allocate and control tokens in the same way as a custom token deployment.

A standard Pump.fun launch uses Pump's bonding-curve launch and migration flow. Tokens available on the curve and the liquidity migrated at graduation are governed by Pump's current protocol configuration; the project must not assume that unsold creator tokens automatically become a separate treasury or airdrop reserve. Confirm the exact launch supply, curve reserves, graduation mechanics, fees, and any available creator/holder-reward mode against the official docs and launch UI immediately before launch.

Official references:
- https://pump.fun/docs/bonding-curve
- https://pump.fun/docs/fees
- https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs

## Revised tokenomics principles

1. **Use the standard Pump.fun launch.** Do not promise a custom pre-launch token allocation that the launch mechanism does not support.
2. **Do not budget a separate 15% liquidity allocation by default.** Pump.fun's bonding curve and graduation flow provide the launch liquidity mechanism. This does not guarantee strong liquidity, buyers, price stability, or trading volume.
3. **No personal liquidity obligation.** The project will not promise to buy tokens or provide external liquidity using the founder's personal funds.
4. **Points first, token rewards only when funded.** Before launch, Climboria may track off-chain CLIMB Points for game/community participation. Points are not tokens, are not transferable, have no guaranteed monetary value, and do not guarantee a future airdrop unless separate official terms explicitly establish one.
5. **A real token airdrop requires actual tokens.** Under a standard Pump.fun launch, tokens for an airdrop may need to be acquired after launch, or a different launch/distribution architecture would need to be chosen. Do not advertise a pre-launch token airdrop until the actual mint, supply route, wallet distribution method, and funding are verified.
6. **Use creator fees cautiously.** If eligible creator fees are received, the project may publish a transparent budget for development and community rewards. Fees are variable and depend on trading activity and the applicable Pump fee rules; never promise a fixed amount or guaranteed yield.
7. **Keep game utility first.** Start with game participation, points, levels, Forge, badges, and community activity. Add on-chain rewards only after the token and distribution mechanics are operational and reviewed.
8. **Base is later.** Any Base deployment or cross-chain representation requires separate supply accounting and a documented issuance/bridge model.

## Allocation framework (replaces the earlier percentage pie chart)

Because standard Pump.fun launch mechanics do not provide the same arbitrary pre-minted allocation buckets as a custom token deployment, the old percentage allocation table is **retired as a launch commitment**. The following are budget categories, not promises that a percentage of tokens is reserved or controlled by Climboria:

| Category | Revised treatment |
|---|---|
| Launch supply and initial liquidity | Governed by Pump.fun's current standard launch and graduation mechanism; verify live settings before launch. |
| Pre-launch community campaign | Off-chain points, quests, referrals, and eligibility tracking only; no claim that points are tokens. |
| Airdrop | Not guaranteed at launch. Announce only after a real token source, allocation, eligibility rules, and distribution method are confirmed. |
| Gameplay rewards | Start with points and non-transferable game progression; token rewards require a funded and transparent source. |
| Development and operations | Fund only from disclosed project resources or actual realized revenue; no assumed token treasury. |
| Marketing and partnerships | Spend only from an explicitly funded budget; no implied token allocation. |
| Team compensation | No on-chain team allocation or vesting claim under the standard launch unless a real, disclosed token source and schedule are established. |
| Future ecosystem / DEX / Base | Roadmap only; no token reserve or funding should be implied before it exists. |

## Reward plan

### Phase 1 — Before launch
- Track eligible activity and CLIMB Points.
- Publish anti-bot rules and a snapshot/eligibility methodology if a campaign is run.
- State clearly that points do not guarantee a token allocation.
- Do not ask users for seed phrases or private keys.

### Phase 2 — After launch
- Review actual available funding and community needs.
- If an airdrop is funded, publish the amount, wallet eligibility, snapshot, claim/distribution method, deadline, and unclaimed-token policy before opening claims.
- If the project uses creator fees for rewards, disclose that the amount is variable and publish receipts and distributions.
- Never buy tokens with borrowed or essential personal/family funds.

### Phase 3 — Later
- Consider a dedicated audited claim program or other distribution mechanism only if scale justifies its cost and security requirements.
- Consider Base only after the Solana supply and any cross-chain issuance model are documented.

## Launch gates

Do not announce a guaranteed TGE date, airdrop amount, claim date, official mint, or price. Before launch, verify:
- the exact official token mint and ticker availability;
- Pump.fun's current token supply, curve reserves, migration and fee configuration;
- whether the selected launch supports any intended holder-reward setting;
- the official campaign terms and anti-bot methodology;
- the source of tokens for any promised distribution;
- official website/social links and scam warnings.

**Planning dates previously discussed:** campaign target November 1, 2026; Solana TGE target window November 8–14, 2026. These remain targets only, not guarantees. Do not publish them as firm commitments unless launch readiness is confirmed.

## Superseded draft

The earlier 1B-token percentage split (20% community/airdrop, 20% gameplay, 15% liquidity, 15% treasury, 10% development, 10% team, 5% marketing, 3% partnerships, 2% security) is superseded for the standard Pump.fun launch model. Do not use those percentages in marketing or as a claim that those tokens are reserved, funded, or under project control.
