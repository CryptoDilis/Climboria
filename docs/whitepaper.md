## 1. Executive summary

Climboria is a game-first browser climbing game and community ecosystem designed to combine skill-based gameplay, progression, crafting, competition, social play and optional blockchain ownership. The core game is intended to be accessible on mobile and desktop browsers without requiring a download, a purchase, or a crypto wallet.

The project follows one guiding principle: **Game First, Speculation Later.** Players should be able to enjoy the game and make meaningful progress without buying digital assets. Blockchain features are planned as optional extensions—not prerequisites for core gameplay.

The long-term ecosystem may include collectible and craftable items, NFT minting and trading, seasons and leaderboards, guild events, a marketplace, a swap interface, a decentralized exchange (DEX), and a project Launchpad. These systems will be introduced in phases and only when their technical, security, operational, liquidity and applicable compliance requirements are ready.

## 2. Vision and principles

Climboria aims to build a lasting game and community rather than a token-first promotion. Product decisions should follow these principles:

- **Accessible gameplay:** core play should not require crypto knowledge, a wallet, or spending.
- **Skill and progression:** competitive outcomes should follow published game rules and verified performance, not first-come-first-served participation.
- **Optional ownership:** blockchain features are optional; players can use eligible items in-game without minting them.
- **Transparent rules:** fees, item eligibility, reward criteria, token details, sale terms and changes should be disclosed before users commit.
- **Responsible rewards:** no reward is guaranteed until the relevant system is implemented, rules are published and funds or assets are available.
- **Security before expansion:** do not rush token, trading, bridge, Launchpad or wallet integrations before review and testing.

## 3. The game

Climboria is planned as a 3D browser climbing experience for mobile and desktop. Players navigate a challenging mountain using aim-and-grab climbing mechanics, tools, timing and movement. The intended launch scope is a focused set of ten quality playable levels, with expansion toward additional levels over time.

Planned gameplay systems include:
- Level progression and increasing difficulty.
- Climbing tools and equipment.
- Collectible ingredients and crafting.
- Forge/refining to upgrade or transform eligible items.
- Skins and cosmetic customization.
- Daily streaks, challenges and events.
- Leaderboards and seasonal competition.
- Social features and guild-based activities.

The design goal is to keep free progression viable. Players should be able to earn ingredients through play and use crafting or Forge systems without being required to purchase equipment. Refining may consume ingredients permanently; the cost and result should be disclosed before confirmation. Optional paid items must not silently invalidate fair competition. Any gameplay-affecting attributes require clear rules and balancing to limit pay-to-win advantages.

## 4. Free-to-play and play-to-earn

Climboria is designed as **free-to-play**, with a planned **play-to-earn** layer. “Play-to-earn” describes a possible reward model, not guaranteed income. Rewards may depend on game performance, eligibility, event rules, available funding, anti-cheat review, token launch readiness and applicable restrictions.

CLIMB Points are intended as off-chain progression points. They are not currently a token, are not freely transferable, and do not guarantee cash, tokens, NFTs, a future token distribution, or any financial value. Campaigns, giveaways and event prizes should only be announced when funded and accompanied by clear eligibility, timing, scoring and payout rules.

## 5. Items, Forge and NFTs

The intended rule is that **items earned or obtained through gameplay can be eligible for NFT minting**, subject to published item rules, technical support and applicable restrictions.

- Items may be used in-game without minting.
- When an item is minted as an NFT and supported by the marketplace, it may be eligible to sell or trade.
- Items that are not minted remain in-game items and are not transferable through NFT marketplaces.
- Climboria plans not to charge a platform minting fee; the player would pay the blockchain network gas fee. Network fees vary and cannot be guaranteed.
- Ownership, rarity, item stats, transfer limits, mint eligibility and marketplace support must be clearly disclosed.
- The initial free starter item is intended to support onboarding; its final minting and transfer treatment must be published in the item rules before the NFT system opens.

NFTs can fluctuate in value and may be illiquid. Minting an item does not guarantee buyers, resale value, or marketplace availability.

## 6. Marketplace and fees

The planned marketplace is intended to support eligible digital items and NFTs. A very small buy/sell marketplace tax is proposed, but the exact rate, calculation basis, exemptions, recipient allocation and applicable transaction types are **not finalized**. These terms must be public before trading opens.

A previous ecosystem proposal allocates:
- **3% of eligible marketplace sales** toward season rewards.
- **2% of eligible marketplace sales** toward events and community.

These are proposals based on eligible marketplace sales, not percentages of token supply or general token trading volume. The final fee basis, exclusions, anti-wash-trading measures, accounting, reward eligibility and distribution rules must be documented before the program begins. Marketplace tax, DEX trading fees, token buy/sell taxes and blockchain gas are different charges and must never be presented as the same fee.

## 7. Seasons, rankings and rewards

Climboria plans competitive seasons with the top 100 ranks visible from the first day. Filling all 100 positions should not automatically end a season. A fixed calendar season with an announced start and end, a defined cutoff and a review window is the proposed approach; final dates and rules must be published before a season starts.

Ranking is intended to reward performance rather than arrival order. The exact scoring formula—including completion time, attempts, tie-breakers, valid-run rules, retries, disqualifications and anti-cheat review—must be published before competitive rewards begin.

Season One reward concept:
- **Ranks 1–10:** numbered badges, with rank-specific benefits to be disclosed in advance.
- **Ranks 11–100:** proposed free ingredients or token rewards, subject to published rules, available funding and official token-launch readiness.

No badge benefit, token reward or prize should be described as guaranteed until the reward system, funding and conditions are confirmed. Results may be reviewed for cheating, botting, exploit abuse and invalid runs before distribution.

## 8. Guilds and community

Players are planned to be able to create or join guilds, cooperate in special events, complete team challenges and compete for guild rewards. Each event should publish its schedule, eligibility, team size, scoring, reward pool, anti-abuse rules and dispute process before participation begins.

Community growth will focus on gameplay challenges, funded campaigns, creator collaborations, guild events and organic community activity. No unfunded prize should be advertised.

## 9. Climboria Launchpad

The planned Launchpad may let external projects apply to run token launches through Climboria. Applications will not be automatically approved. The intended review process may examine project disclosures, team and official links, token configuration, tokenomics, authority controls, security evidence, distribution plans and liquidity arrangements.

Before any sale opens, its project should publish:
- Token supply and distribution.
- Sale price or pricing method, schedule and eligibility.
- Allocation method, wallet limits and oversubscription rules.
- Vesting, lockups and unlock schedules.
- Platform and transaction fees.
- Intended use of proceeds and liquidity plan.
- Cancellation and refund conditions, where applicable.
- Material risks, conflicts of interest and region restrictions.

Launchpad review is not a guarantee that a project is safe, legitimate or profitable. A Launchpad sale does not guarantee an exchange listing, liquidity, price appreciation or returns. Final Launchpad fees, sale formats, review criteria and operational controls remain to be defined and published before the first launch. Launches should only proceed after technical, security, operational and applicable legal/compliance checks are ready.

## 10. Swap and Climboria DEX

The near-term concept includes a swap interface; the longer-term vision is a Climboria DEX with a familiar decentralized trading experience. Depending on supported integrations, the interface may let users connect a wallet, choose a token pair, review a quoted exchange rate, slippage tolerance, price impact and network fee, then approve a transaction in their own wallet.

Additional planned features may include charts, liquidity information, transaction history and supported token-pair discovery. The interface must distinguish estimated output from executed results and disclose that prices can move, transactions can fail, liquidity can be limited, and trades can lose value.

A temporary swap is planned after the official Climboria token launches, subject to verified token configuration, supported wallet integration, reliable routing, sufficient liquidity and security checks. The dedicated DEX is a longer-term goal. No official $CLIMB address is currently confirmed in this document, and no interface should invent or imply a verified token contract. Cross-chain support, including any future Base deployment, depends on network-specific security, liquidity and operational readiness.

## 11. Token design and launch status

The preferred ticker candidate is **$CLIMB**, subject to availability and final verification. Solana is the initial intended chain; Base is a later possibility. A one-to-one presence across chains would require an explicitly designed, secured and documented deployment/bridging model—it should not be assumed to exist automatically.

The final token supply, mint address, allocation, vesting, treasury controls, authority settings, launch mechanics and any creator-fee treatment are not confirmed by this draft. A previously discussed supply figure is only a target until the actual launch configuration is finalized. No token should be represented as official without a verified address published through Climboria's official channels.

The token's eventual utility, if launched, should be documented precisely and should not be marketed as a guaranteed investment or source of profit. Users should verify the address and understand volatility, liquidity, smart-contract, wallet, regulatory and market risks before transacting.

## 12. Technical architecture and security

The browser game is being developed with web technologies, including Three.js for 3D rendering. Blockchain integrations are planned as separate layers so core gameplay can remain usable without a wallet. Wallet connection, swap routing, NFT minting, marketplace settlement, Launchpad contracts and cross-chain infrastructure require separate implementation and review.

Security priorities include:
- Secure wallet-approval flows and clear transaction summaries.
- No fabricated balances, prices, holders, liquidity, project metrics or trading activity.
- Token and contract address verification through official sources.
- Testing for mobile and desktop browser behavior.
- Review of smart contracts, permissions, minting authorities, treasury controls and upgrade mechanisms where applicable.
- Anti-bot, anti-sybil, anti-cheat and wash-trading protections.
- Incident response, user disclosures and clear support/reporting channels.
- Independent review appropriate to the risk and scale of each on-chain feature.

No audit or security certification is claimed by this document. An interface or code review alone is not a guarantee of safety.

## 13. Roadmap

Dates below are targets, not guarantees. Delivery may move if gameplay quality, testing, security, liquidity, compliance or operational readiness requires more time.

| Phase | Target / scope |
|---|---|
| Core game | Stabilize the browser experience and deliver an initial set of ten quality playable levels. |
| Progression and community | Expand crafting/Forge, leaderboards, seasons, challenges, guilds and community tools. |
| NFT and marketplace systems | Define item eligibility, minting, trading, fee disclosures and ownership rules; test before release. |

| Broader ecosystem | Target core ecosystem delivery before the end of 2026, subject to readiness. |

Solana is the initial chain focus. Base and additional networks are future possibilities, not guaranteed launch-day support. A dedicated mobile app is also planned for the future; the current product direction is browser-first.

## 14. Risks and limitations

Climboria may face technical delays, bugs, cheating, low user adoption, market volatility, limited liquidity, third-party service changes, wallet risks, smart-contract vulnerabilities, regulatory uncertainty and changing network fees. NFTs and tokens may lose some or all of their value. A market may not exist for a particular asset. Rewards can be delayed, changed or unavailable if published conditions or funding requirements are not met.

Users should never spend money they cannot afford to lose, rely on projected rewards as income, share seed phrases, or trust unofficial token addresses or direct messages. Official announcements should identify what is live, what is planned and what remains under review.
