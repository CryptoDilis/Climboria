## Owner direction — Climboria Points (CP), marketplace and item ownership — 2026-11-28 PHT

**Approved design direction from owner:** replace proposed Climboria Gold with CP (Climboria Points). Limited global CP supply; UI displays unmined reserve and circulating supply from authoritative records. CP is earned through gameplay; spend CP for fishing bait, enhancement, forging, NPC items, crafting armor/weapons and related services. Consumed CP returns to unmined reserve, not permanently burned and not paid to an owner wallet. No direct player-to-player item transfer/gifting; ownership changes through Climboria Marketplace only. Owner requests marketplace sale of CP and optional tokenization of all item categories including CP. No final pricing, conversion contract or financial release approved; subsequent owner decision below fixes the maximum ceiling and marketplace settlement asset. CP is separate from official CLIMB, KOS settlement and Treasure Vault. Owner delegates choice of additional Treasure Vault funding sources for player experience; recommendation: launch with owner deposits and approved KOS fee only; optional voluntary donations considered later, no perks, prize odds or solicitation, no live payment path.

**Proposed accounting (review before implementation):** durable server-controlled relational CP ledger (e.g. isolated PostgreSQL/Supabase schema), not a crypto wallet, with immutable journal and atomic balance/reserve movements, server-only authorization, idempotency keys, integer accounting, audited corrections and backup/reconciliation. Global max = unmined reserve + spendable CP + reserved/escrowed CP + CP backing outstanding on-chain representations; mutually exclusive states, no double counting; circulating = max minus unmined reserve (show encumbered balances separately). No negative balances, admin unlimited issuance, NPC balance fabrication, direct client writes or offline earn. Earn moves reserve to player; NPC/service spend returns CP to reserve; marketplace transfer does not change circulating supply unless an approved CP fee is recycled. Failed transaction leaves balances unchanged. No reserve depletion promise; budget issuance by validated activity and active population. Historical illustrative 100-million example superseded by the immutable 1-billion maximum below.

**Historical suggested playtest earn/spend values (synthetic only; not approved; earn rates superseded by lower proposals below):** ordinary chest 5–20 CP, verified fishing 2–8 CP per eligible completion, validated gathering 1–5 CP, quest 20–100 CP, exploration milestones 10–50 CP, rare events 50–200 CP; bait 2–5 CP, basic NPC consumables 5–25 CP, repairs 10–50 CP, crafting 25–250 CP plus materials, enhancements 50–500 CP plus materials. No forced payment/wallet for ordinary play; guests can explore using isolated non-redeemable preview progression.

## Owner decision — immutable 1 billion CP maximum, scarce earning and CLIMB-only marketplace — 2026-11-28 PHT

**Approved design decisions:** maximum global CP supply is exactly 1,000,000,000 CP. This is an immutable lifetime ceiling, NOT a fixed circulating supply or an instruction to issue all CP. Even the owner, administrators, future governance or migration/upgrade paths must have no authority to increase the ceiling or create additional CP above it. The circulating amount varies as players earn and spend CP. Gameplay acquisition must be difficult and meaningful. Climboria Marketplace purchases/sales of player-listed CP and eligible items/NFTs are denominated and settled exclusively in official Base CLIMB; no CP, ETH, USDC or other assets as marketplace payment. Network gas is separate. NPC and routine game services remain CP-priced, with consumed CP returned to the unmined reserve. No direct player-to-player item transfer. No guaranteed exchange rate, buyback, price or liquidity.

**Proposed accounting and cap enforcement:** initialize the reserve accounting capacity at 1,000,000,000 unmined CP, zero circulating, subject to reviewed launch. Immutable cap enforced in trusted ledger and every issuance/migration/reconciliation path; no editable cap setting, admin grant bypass, reset-to-full or unrestricted correction. Database superusers can technically alter records, so database-only enforcement cannot honestly guarantee absolute immutability against privileged infrastructure compromise. Require independent append-only accounting/checkpoint verification, separated credentials, fail-closed reconciliation, reviewed migration conservation, and an independently verifiable immutable cap mechanism before promising cryptographic immutability; any later on-chain representation must preserve the global cap and exclusive ownership, not create extra CP. Proposed supply equation: cap = unmined reserve + spendable + reserved/escrowed + backing of outstanding on-chain representations. Buckets mutually exclusive; circulating = sum of non-reserve buckets. Gameplay earning debits reserve; service spend credits reserve; marketplace transfer conserves circulating CP. No actual issuance or balance implied.

**Proposed scarcity balancing, NOT final rates:** ordinary fishing/gathering yields materials primarily, not CP per action. Suggested test-only CP awards: common chest 0–1, uncommon 0–2, rare 1–3, epic 3–5, legendary 5–10, mythic 10–20; substantial verified fishing/gathering milestone 1–2, first exploration milestone 2–5, quest 3–10, difficult event 10–25. These supersede earlier high-rate proposals for discussion only. Validate global issuance and consumption by active population, first-completion evidence, account/activity budgets, diminishing repeat gains, bot/replay/collusion denial and accessibility. Preserve ordinary items/XP even when CP is not awarded; avoid forced purchases and punitive streaks. Free players must be able to make routine progress. Exact odds, earn rates, precision, service prices and scarcity budgets require simulation and approval.

**Financial release gate retained:** tradeable gameplay-earned CP/items may create indirect financial rewards. Explicit reconciliation with current V4 no-automatic-crypto-rewards policy, economic/legal/security review, exact official token verification, authoritative ownership and separate release approval remain necessary before real settlement or tokenization. No Base policy/contracts changed in this Game-only record. Next discuss marketplace fee rate/payer/destination, NFT custody and transfer rules, outstanding chest balancing and KOS collection details.
