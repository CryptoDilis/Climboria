# Climboria Roadmap

> Planning reference. Dates and features are targets, not guarantees. A feature is not live until implemented, tested, and officially announced.

## Launch scope — Jarrell’s Peak only

Jarrell’s Peak is the sole planned launch mountain: a vast, endless-feeling wilderness for survival, exploration, fishing, swimming, and treasure hunting. It has no summit or final level. Documentation work must not change existing gameplay mechanics.

### Stage 1 — Protect the current game
- Preserve movement, collision, swimming, fishing, controls, camera, sound, weather, and world interactions.
- Verify the riverside spawn camp, long river, shoreline, treasure discovery, death/respawn, and desktop/mobile stability.
- Align the website, Overview, Whitepaper, Game Concept, and root README.

### Stage 2 — Economy readiness
- Marketplace intended to use $CLIMB only for eligible items and crafted gear.
- Buy fee: 2.5%; sell fee: 2.5%.
- Allocate 5% of actual collected marketplace fee revenue to the game funding pool; this is not an extra 5% of trading volume.
- Secure accounting, wallet integration, inventory persistence, anti-cheat, and published rules are prerequisites.
- NFT minting is intended to have no Climboria minting fee; player pays network gas.

### Stage 3 — Top Gainers
- Permanent All-Time and separate Weekly Top Gainers boards.
- Score combines eligible $CLIMB earnings, eligible treasure value, and disclosed rarity/difficulty bonuses.
- Weekly payout target, if funded and launched: Friday 03:00 UTC / 11:00 AM Philippines time.
- No summit ranking and no badges in the current launch plan.
- No rewards without a verified funded pool.

### Stage 4 — Chatworld
- Global Chat, Proximity Chat, and Private Messages in Jarrell’s Peak and Arena.
- Mute, block, report, anti-spam controls, and moderator tools.
- Server-side identity, permissions, delivery, and moderation testing required.

### Stage 5 — Public Arena (future map)
- Direct access from the game menu via an ARENA button.
- Large forest setting, central platform, sponsor flag, event banners, and entrance sponsor panel.
- Public access at any time.
- Free-for-all when no event is running; no rewards for casual fights.
- Guild Battle makes the whole Arena a battlefield for two approved guilds; other players spectate.
- Any guild member can request a battle; guild leaders approve.
- Target one shared world with 1,000+ concurrent players, subject to architecture and load testing.
- Weapons must fit the climbing theme; detailed weapon design is deferred.

### Stage 6 — Sponsorship operations
- Admin approval and written eligibility checklist.
- Launchpad-created tokens may use fast-track review with basic checks; other crypto sponsors receive full review; non-crypto sponsors are reviewed according to risk.
- Full sponsorship fee and entire promised prize pool must be received and verified before any announcement, branding, or event activation.
- Announce approved events on X.com, homepage, game menu, and running in-game announcements.
- Sponsor placements may include central flag, Arena entrance panel, event banners, homepage, game menu, and in-game announcements.
- Written agreement must define exclusivity, period, price, auto-renewal, cancellation, refund, postponement, force majeure, and prize protection.
- Renewal grace period: 3 days; pause new announcements/activations while unpaid. If unpaid after the grace period, sponsorship ends according to the agreement.

### Stage 7 — Future Guild Wars and wagers
- Weekly Guild Wars with rewards only after budget is available and the feature is implemented.
- Casual fights are free.
- Optional wager matches are planned with mutually agreed stakes; 2.5% of the total pot goes to game funding, with the remainder paid to the winner.
- Secure escrow, consent, disconnect/dispute handling, anti-abuse safeguards, and compliance review are prerequisites.

### Stage 8 — Further expansion
- Additional maps are deferred until after token launch and a budget review.
- Any future badge system is deferred.
- Base chain integration and dedicated mobile app remain future plans until released.

## Release gate

Never describe a future feature as live just because it appears in a mock-up or has a code commit. Verify functionality, security, funding, accessibility, and desktop/mobile behavior before announcing release.
