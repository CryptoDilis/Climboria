## What is the project about?

Climboria is a browser-based gaming and digital-asset ecosystem centered on an exploration and survival adventure called Jarrell’s Peak. The project combines a free-to-play game concept with planned supporting services for token discovery and swapping, a decentralized exchange interface, a project launch platform, and a marketplace for eligible in-game items. Its design emphasizes accessible gameplay for users who do not need a crypto wallet to enter the core game, while blockchain functions are intended for optional transactions and digital ownership features. Solana is the initial blockchain focus, with a possible Base expansion planned for a later phase. Product components are being developed and released in stages; this description does not imply that every feature, token market, reward, or blockchain integration is currently operational.

## What makes your project unique?

Climboria’s distinguishing approach is its combination of an accessible browser game and a broader, separately gated Web3 ecosystem. Jarrell’s Peak is designed as an endless wilderness survival and exploration experience rather than a conventional sequence of short levels, with environmental discovery, resource gathering, fishing, swimming, hidden treasure locations, and risk-based exploration forming its intended gameplay loop. The project also separates the game from optional financial interactions: basic game access is intended to be free and not require a wallet, while token swaps, marketplace activity, and digital-item ownership are planned as distinct services. Climboria documents release gates and aims to identify planned features clearly rather than presenting interfaces or roadmap concepts as live products.

## History of your project?

Climboria began as a browser-first climbing game concept and developed into a larger gaming and digital-asset ecosystem. Its early product direction focused on a physics-based climbing experience designed to run in a web browser on desktop and mobile devices. The game concept subsequently expanded to include Jarrell’s Peak, an open-world survival and exploration environment, alongside ideas for crafting, player progression, treasure discovery, and community features. The project’s scope later incorporated a Solana-focused token plan and proposed Swap, DEX, Launchpad, and marketplace services. Development has been organized around a staged delivery process, with production integrations and verification required before individual services are described as operational. The project’s public website and source repository provide its current product and development references.

## What's next for your project?

Climboria’s next steps follow a staged engineering plan. The current priority order is to complete and verify a production Swap transaction lifecycle, advance the DEX with reliable market data and accurate token identification, make the intended login methods operational, and then complete the full playable game release. The Launchpad is planned as the final priority after those foundations. Separately, the project intends to improve the game’s progression and economy systems, and to evaluate marketplace, optional NFT minting, rewards, social features, and a later Base expansion. Each release is dependent on implementation, security checks, provider availability, and end-to-end testing. Roadmap targets are planning information, not guaranteed delivery dates.

## What can your coin/token be used for?

$CLIMB is the intended utility token for the Climboria ecosystem, with Solana as the initial chain focus. Under the documented design, it is intended to serve as the payment currency for eligible marketplace ingredients, crafted equipment, and other supported digital items. It may also be used in selected game-economy functions, community reward programs, and future ecosystem services if those features are implemented and their rules are published. The current marketplace design proposes a 2.5% buy fee and a 2.5% sell fee; five percent of marketplace fee revenue actually collected is intended for the game funding pool. These are design specifications, not confirmation of an active marketplace or fee contract. Token address, supply, authorities, distribution, active markets, and live utility must be verified from official on-chain and project sources before being stated as facts.
