What to include in your report

Providing clear details can help the team investigate without needing access to your account or funds.

  • Which feature is affected: Login, DEX, Swap, Launchpad, Game, or other.
  • What happened, the error message, and steps to reproduce it.
  • Approximate date, time, and your timezone.
  • If relevant, a public blockchain transaction signature and network.
  • A screenshot with sensitive information removed, if helpful.

Security: protect your wallet

Climboria Support will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, password, one-time code, or payment to unlock support. Do not share wallet recovery phrases, credentials, or signed transactions.

Be careful of impersonators and unsolicited messages. Confirm that any account contacting you matches the official @ClimboriaHQ profile. Never connect a wallet to an unfamiliar link sent by someone claiming to be support.

For a missing deposit or transaction issue, check the public transaction signature on a trusted explorer. Do not share private account credentials.
