What $CLIMB is for

$CLIMB is planned to connect Climboria’s game and platforms. In-game, it may be used for eligible purchases, forging, upgrades, and marketplace activity as those features are introduced.

On the Climboria DEX, we plan to explore utility tied to trading features and platform fees. On the Launchpad, $CLIMB may support eligible launch services and features for creators.

We’re building the game, DEX, and Launchpad step by step. Specific token uses, fee benefits, and requirements will be announced once they’re confirmed and implemented.
