Agreed fee design · not yet a live system

Marketplace: 2.5% buy fee and 2.5% sell fee. Five percent of the actual marketplace fee revenue collected is allocated to the Climboria game funding pool; this is not an additional 5% charge on trading volume.

Future Arena wagers: optional wager matches would charge a 2.5% platform fee on the total pot, sent to the game funding pool. Casual fights remain free and have no rewards.

Rewards: Jarrell’s Peak Top Gainers rewards can be paid only from a verified, funded pool under published rules. No rewards are guaranteed before launch readiness and funding.

Marketplace items and eligible crafted gear are intended to use $CLIMB only. NFT minting is intended to have no Climboria minting fee; players pay network gas. These features are not live until implemented and verified.
