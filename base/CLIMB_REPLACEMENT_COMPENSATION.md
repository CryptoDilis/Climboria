# CLIMB Base-first replacement — capped legacy compensation proposal

2026-11-26 UTC. Internal design candidate, not an approved token launch, snapshot, allocation, claim offer or price commitment. This proposal advances Option B in [migration design](CLIMB_MIGRATION_DESIGN.md), prompted by the founder's concern about ongoing Solana purchases and cross-chain price divergence. Continuing design is not approval to issue tokens.

## Why not an open-ended conversion?
The original Token-2022 mint has historical null mint/freeze authorities. Legacy trades may continue even if Climboria removes official utility. If Base trades at a premium, an open-ended fixed 1:1 conversion creates a potential buy-cheap/claim/sell route against Base liquidity. A source burn would conserve units but would not prevent price-divergence arbitrage. Neither prices nor future demand are guaranteed. Do not assume the curve can be stopped, withdrawn, or burned.

The alternative is a distinct Base replacement economy with a finite, one-time legacy compensation budget, no permanent conversion or reverse bridge. It is NOT a supply-conserving bridge: the Solana supply may continue to exist and trade. Publicly disclose both contracts and supplies, legacy utility changes, risks, eligibility and non-convertibility before any launch; never describe them as one conserved supply.

## Proposed compensation rules (all require review)
- Establish a defensible, independently validated historical cutoff and public rationale; no open-ended entitlement from purchases after cutoff. Evaluate reasonable notice, good-faith purchases made before notice, transfer histories, custody and appeals. No secret preferential allocations or retroactive changes after final approval without transparent review. Do not announce a date, ratio or promise before evidence and terms are ready.
- Define eligible beneficial holders under published rules; reconcile token accounts to authorities and resolve custodial accounts, frozen holdings, delegates and curve/LP claims. Prove founder holdings and disclose founder treatment separately; exclude founder holdings from the external-holder pool if an independently approved policy provides a separate allocation. No confiscation of unrelated holders' assets.
- Set a fixed compensation budget C in raw Base token units before claims. For a simple pro-rata model, E is the sum of verified eligible legacy raw units; each eligible entitlement w receives floor(C*w/E), with E>0, w>=0, unique eligibility IDs, and sum(w)=E. Use integer arithmetic, no floating point. Cap aggregate distributed compensation at C. If E=0, distribute nothing. Rounding dust and unclaimed allocations are not automatically paid to founder or treasury. Publish disposition before activation (prefer burn of unclaimed reserve after disclosed claim and appeal windows, subject to review).
- No live price oracle, automatic 1:1 rate, dynamic entitlement based on purchases after cutoff, or additional mint on compensation burn. Allocation is a one-time finite grant and not an exchange quote or promise to recover loss. Consider documented valuations and alternatives before selecting compensation size; never imply equivalent market value.
- Use an independently reviewed, fixed-funded claim mechanism rather than unlimited minting: exact immutable allocation manifest or independently reproduced Merkle root, chain/version/contract-bound proof of destination authorization, verified source-wallet ownership without requesting seed phrase, unique claim IDs, replay prevention and cumulative cap. One claim per approved entitlement, multiple token accounts reconciled. No automatic transfer of entitlement with legacy token trades. Dispute or recovery procedures for lost access and custodial claims require explicit policy.
- No forced legacy burn. If a voluntary surrender requirement is later selected, first test recoverable Base claim and finalized retirement proof; disclose irreversibility and never demand surrender before safe verified claim is available. No funds or signatures now.

## Base supply accounting
Let S be approved fixed initial Base issuance and C, L, G, T, O be independently approved allocations for compensation, liquidity, gameplay rewards, treasury and other disclosed obligations respectively. All nonnegative integer raw units; C+L+G+T+O=S. C is included in S, not an extra mint. Keep compensation funded and segregated from liquidity and gameplay obligations. Total supply after verified Base burns is S-B, 0<=B<=S. Claim payout is a transfer from reserve and must not change totalSupply. Base burns never replenish issuance allowance. Solana burns are separate and do not imply Base economic burns.

No S, C, ratio, percentages, vesting, cutoff or deadlines selected. Preserve the existing testnet burn prototype; use the verified historical test burn only as evidence of standard ERC-20 burn mechanics, not proof of production claim security. No public tokenomics update until approved.
