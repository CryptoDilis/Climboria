# CLIMB utility and Signature governance — proposed policy

2026-11-27 UTC. Owner requested adding utility + governance, staking eligibility, proposal requirements and incentive funding. DESIGN_ONLY / MAINNET_NO_GO. Numerical parameters below are recommendations, not final approval, a yield promise, or a live feature.

## Eligibility and voting
- Existing proposed Base ERC-20 CLIMB, 888,888,888 initial units, 6 decimals. No second token, additional issuance or change to seven master allocations. Staking means escrow of existing transferable tokens, not Base validator staking.
- Voter: at least 1,000 CLIMB in a non-transferable stake position with original lock >=90 days and age >=30 days. Proposer: at least 25,000 CLIMB with original lock >=180 days and age >=30 days. Only qualifying principal counts, one CLIMB = one vote; no time multiplier. Stake must remain locked through the scheduled end of the vote; eligible cohort and weight frozen using a reviewed checkpoint/snapshot before proposal creation. No instant borrowing, newly added stake or double voting. Stake age applies to each deposit; extending lock does not backdate new principal. Principal claim after agreed unlock, no confiscation, no early exit by default. No reward dependency on withdrawal.
- Proposal: public rationale, requested action, impact, conflicts and estimated costs; 48-hour discussion before 7-day vote. One live proposal per proposer; spam review with transparent reasons. Example quorum >=10% of all snapshot-eligible staked voting power; approval strictly >50% of Yes+No, abstain counts only toward quorum. No vote if eligible supply zero; require a reviewed minimum eligible community size and concentration analysis before activation. Thresholds provisional and must be modeled against actual circulating supply, not total issuance. No promise of one-person-one-vote; token concentration remains possible. Exclude unissued/locked project reserves, vesting and custody assets, related-party-controlled project funds; disclose founder/team personal votes and require conflict review. Wallet caps alone do not prevent Sybil.
- Advisory only initially: launchpad feature/fee recommendations, ecosystem priorities, community events and grant recommendations. No execution authority, treasury withdrawal, mint, upgrade, signer activation or bypass of reserve caps. Publish outcome and response. Later binding actions need separate approval, audited execution policy, applicable timelock and Safe authorization. No governance activation merely because solo bootstrap exists.

## Utility and funding
- Intended game/marketplace utility remains subject to existing feature gates. Proposed governance perks: voting, qualified proposal access, eligible launchpad discounts or priority access where reviewed and funded; no guarantee of launchpad allocation, discount or returns.
- Start with zero monetary staking rewards. Optional finite incentives: proposed maximum 0.25% of initial supply = 2,222,222.220000 CLIMB, carved ONLY from existing 5% qualified launchpad incentives/development sub-budget within 15% Ecosystem & Launchpad; remaining 4.75% = 42,222,222.180000 CLIMB. No additional mint, gaming/marketing/legacy reallocation or founder pilot funds. This sub-split is not approved for spending or changes to public tokenomics.
- Proposed 24 model months (30 days each) maximum 92,592.592500 CLIMB/month subject to funding and original ecosystem release cap/period; no automatic release or carry-forward of unused monthly allocation. Fund a reviewed distinct reward distributor via approved reserve path (current expense fixture is not that distributor), 3-of-5 approvals, applicable 48-hour delay, exact budget and unique expense ID. No monetary rewards during solo founder phase. No borrowing against future fees; actual net fees may support separate future benefits only after accounting, legal and policy review. No guaranteed APY or ongoing obligation after program expiry.
- Distribute actual funded monthly amount pro rata by qualifying time-weighted stake in that month, not votes cast or vote direction; no duplicate claim, round down to 6 decimals, bounded claims, define expiry and locked residual/dust disposition before funding. Example only: funded 10,000 CLIMB/month, 1,000,000 total qualifying time-weighted stake and 10,000 stake for entire period => 100 CLIMB reward. Variable; zero funding => zero reward. Principal escrow segregated from incentives and treasury, no lending/reinvestment/slashing/admin sweep; test conservation and withdrawals even with rewards paused or exhausted. Review checkpointing, replay, reentrancy, accounting, lock expiry, proposal snapshot and concentration; no production changes or funds now.
