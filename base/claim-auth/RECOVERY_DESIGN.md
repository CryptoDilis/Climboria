# Claim recovery boundary — synthetic prototype only

2026-11-26 UTC. This is a design checkpoint, not a deployed service, approved entitlement, or recovery promise.

Prefer direct recipient-submitted claim to a single independently reviewed immutable contract per approved campaign. No automatic retries, relayer or custody. Before any wallet prompt, independently reproduce manifest and verify source entitlement, recipient control, chain, exact contract/token runtime, funded reserve, deadline and destination authorization. Show human-readable terms; no seed phrases or blind signatures.

Persist approved campaign/manifest version and entitlement ID, exact source authorization digest and destination, issued challenge nonce, evidence and transaction references in a durable transactional store. Unique constraints for (campaign, entitlement) and (campaign, nonce). Challenge verification and reservation must commit atomically before any claim handoff; reservation is not proof of payout. Do not treat an expired challenge or missing receipt as an entitlement reset. Restrict access, encrypt sensitive evidence, publish retention policy and audit changes. Do not log signatures or secret credentials.

If the claimant loses connectivity, recover by reading the approved contract's finalized claimed flag and exact entitlement tuple; locate the canonical successful receipt and match claim event, token transfer and historical state. Bind chain, contract, token, manifest, recipient, amount, finalized block and transaction; independently validate provider provenance and runtime. Receipt success alone is insufficient. Pending/unknown/reorg or inconsistent data: freeze automated progression; investigate. Finalized revert or unclaimed flag does not alone exclude another pending transaction. No automatic resubmission. Manual review may permit an owner-signed retry of the same immutable entitlement only after reconciling all pending evidence. On-chain one-time guard prevents duplicate payout within that contract, not across multiple deployments. Never deploy a replacement claim contract or reset paid state without full reconciliation and reviewed migration; cross-instance replay remains unresolved.

After deadline, unresolved claims require published appeal/recovery policy and separate reviewed remediation; do not silently confiscate or auto-remint. Prototype closure burns unspent reserve at immutable deadline, so production must resolve appeals and define notice before any irreversible burn. No actual holder signature, eligibility, deployment, database, or chain verification in this change. The recovery model accepts synthetic normalized observations only; booleans are not cryptographic evidence. Status MAINNET_NO_GO.
