# Proposed discretionary expense lifecycle authorization — review only

Status: PROPOSED, NOT APPROVED, NOT IMPLEMENTED. 2026-11-28 UTC. Based on Meta F1/F4/F7, Claude F1, Gemini F1 and DeepSeek F-01; exact frozen #1072 and verified test-only #1083. No deployment approval.

## Objective
When an address is nominated as a Safe replacement, pre-existing unpaid discretionary expenses to that address must no longer execute after cancellation or expiry without a new quorum-approved proposal. Preserve unrelated recipients' valid expenses and evidence reuse for unpaid/cancelled proposals. Existing successful owner rotation still invalidates all prior-epoch authorizations. No token allocation, reward entitlement, legacy proof claim, liquidity custody or ownership recovery change. Address-level separation does not establish beneficial ownership.

## Candidate design for review
- Persist per-address monotonic conflict generation, updated atomically only on successful nomination. Record the affected address in each expense proposal with the observed generation; existing proposals with stale generation must revert at execution before accounting or registry consumption. No global epoch increment solely for cancellation or expiry. Every newly nominated address remains blocked throughout the existing live pending interval.
- Bind the exact recipient's expected conflict generation and eligibility phase to the signed Safe expense calldata (new static ABI shape, exact selector and length whitelist) and proposal identity. Reject mismatches and retain one-day signed execution window, nonce, epoch, amount, month, evidence and delay checks. A lifecycle change for recipient X should not invalidate a proposal for unrelated Y unless the actual custody epoch changes.
- Define a distinct post-conflict approval context. Mere rotationSerial matching is insufficient when cancellation/expiry does not change the serial. A schedule/cancel/expiry phase-aware context can distinguish pre-conflict and live-pending intents from ordinary post-conflict intents. Expiry is deterministic, so signers can pre-sign future context values; no contract can prove wall-clock signature creation time from an ECDSA signature. Do not market this as proof that signatures were produced after a conflict. If actual post-event approval is required, require a separately authorized on-chain release/reapproval ceremony and operational signing controls, still acknowledging quorum can preauthorize predictable actions.
- Fresh post-conflict proposals require quorum authorization, a fresh proposal nonce, normal delay, current epoch and caps. Old invalidated proposal IDs must never re-arm when pending state disappears. No evidence consumption until a successful execution. Consider explicit events for conflict generation and stale proposal status; indexer derives live pending from digest plus expiry.
- For an actual swap: keep current owner exclusion and global epoch invalidation. Former owner eligibility after swap remains a separate policy: ordinary new quorum-approved expense vs cooling-off; neither is approved here. Do not change proof-bound historical holder rights merely because of a later role.

## Required regressions before acceptance
1. All three expense vaults: original approved expense rejected while pending, after cancellation and at exact expiry; fresh proposal after cancellation/expiry accepted only with correct context and delay. Evidence unconsumed, balances/caps/nonce rollback.
2. Same-block nomination and proposal ordering, repeat candidacy, superseded pending, failed schedule, distinct unrelated payee unaffected, actual swap and stale epoch rejection.
3. Correctly signed stale/future-nonce calldata for old context, same-serial cancel and expiry, wrong context and wrong ABI lengths; exact authorization-window boundaries. Explicitly distinguish pre-signed future-context residual from unauthorized bypass.
4. Preserve ordinary contract payees, initial reward signer, retirement, legitimate historical proof claims, fixed liquidity custody, supply and shared registry race behavior.
5. Evaluate compatibility across all synthetic custody adapters, source and ABI changes, storage layout, max runtime/initcode, compile 0.8.19 and experimental 0.8.39, exact replays and targeted AI re-review. Do not silently update frozen candidate pins.
