## Decision: one-time migration preferred, conditional on feasibility
Option A (preferred for preserving a single economic supply): opt-in one-way retirement-and-claim. A verified original-token burn or demonstrably irreversible lock precedes an equivalent Base issuance. No permanent two-way bridge, no reverse release and no minting from screenshots. A one-time audited issuance controller with hard cumulative cap, exact entitlement rules, independent proof validation, replay protection, separate narrowly scoped governance and permanently closed minting after claims is required. Unclaimed balances are not automatically minted or diverted. If claim uses reserve pre-mint rather than mint-on-claim, include all reserve tokens in Base totalSupply and backing before issuance. Do not promise this path until independent evidence shows how issuance beyond actual migrated holdings is possible.
Option B (if curve reserves cannot be retired): transparently retire official support of old CLIMB and consider a distinct replacement token economy with independently reviewed capped holder compensation. Old token may remain tradable and its supply remains onchain; this is not a supply-conserving bridge or a 1:1 conversion of the whole old supply. Explicitly disclose parallel legacy supply, economic/holder impact, distribution and legal review. Requires explicit owner decision and published terms before deployment. Never simply issue another 1B while describing both as one fixed-supply token.

## Claim security and holder experience
Public announcement and reasonable claim period; no solicitation of seed phrases or transfers to social-media addresses. Verify current finalized source token account, holder authority, exact mint/program, amount, destination Base address authorization, unique transaction/instruction proof, finality, and no replay. Domain-bind proof to migration version, source/destination chain and exact destination. Prefer independently reviewed proof infrastructure; no self-attested receipt or single unaudited relayer. Resolve custody/vault and post-snapshot transfer rules before activation; protect holders even if they have not seen the announcement. No forced burn or owner action on other balances. Test recovery for a finalized source retirement with an interrupted Base claim before any actual retirement; no irreversible user step until claim path, failure handling and security review are complete.
