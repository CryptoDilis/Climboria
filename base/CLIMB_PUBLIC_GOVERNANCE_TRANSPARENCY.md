# Public governance forum and treasury transparency — staged release
2026-11-27 UTC. Owner requested premium mobile-friendly Bitcointalk-style forum, formal proposals/voting, wallet-only sign-in, homepage public reserve balances and movements. This commit implements read-only navigation and pre-launch presentation only. Posting, wallet login, votes, and live tracking OFF. No fake accounts, balances, posts, votes or transactions.

## Forum trust model
Public reading; verified Base wallet challenge for posting/replies. No email, social, username, guest or connected-wallet-only access. Forum-specific server-issued one-use unpredictable nonce bound to domain, URI, chain 8453, address, issued/expiry time and intended session; validate EIP-4361 signature and replay prevention server-side, enforce chain and signer, reject existing email-only Supabase sessions at protected endpoints. Establish explicit support policy for EIP-1271 contract wallets. Secure session cookies/expiry, account change/logout, CSRF protection and rate limits. No wallet transaction, allowance or private keys for login. Display names are presentation only. Build threads, replies, pagination, moderation/reporting, spam controls, server-side access enforcement and actual multi-user tests before posting. Formal proposals/votes separate from topics; stake thresholds and snapshots provisional; no fund execution from advisory votes.

## Treasury trust model
Seven proposed allocation categories shown separately from unknown actual balances. Official production CA and verified reserve-address registry absent. Future read-only backend uses server-side RPC, finalized canonical block, same-block balanceOf, exact ERC20 Transfer logs and timestamp; verify token, chain and registry; deduplicate chain+tx hash+log index; include incoming/outgoing, mint/burn and internal movements without double counting. Durable cursor, pagination and gap/reorg detection, observed block/time and stale/unavailable states; never substitute 0 for error. Exact raw integer amounts, six decimals, validated BaseScan tx and address links. Publish coverage and refresh lag; 30–60 second polling target only, not guaranteed realtime. Related campaign wallets separately verified; no unrelated personal addresses. No spending/approval capability. No production deployment or financial activity.

## Mandatory wallet-only interaction — clarified by owner
All state-changing forum actions require a server-verified Base wallet session: topic creation, posting, comments, replies, reactions, reports, proposal submission, voting, edits/deletes and moderation. No founder or administrator bypass; privileged actions additionally require a server-managed authorized role. Public read-only browsing remains anonymous. Official proposals and votes additionally require reviewed CLIMB stake eligibility and snapshot rules; ordinary discussions do not require token ownership. No Gmail/email/X/guest/username-only or connected-wallet-only session can authorize any mutation. Never trust a wallet address, role or stake amount supplied by the client. Verify authorization at every mutation endpoint and database boundary; default deny. Test missing, expired, revoked, wrong-chain, replayed and mismatched wallet sessions and email-only sessions for every mutation. Login is message signing, not spending approval. Current page remains disabled pending implementation and verification.
