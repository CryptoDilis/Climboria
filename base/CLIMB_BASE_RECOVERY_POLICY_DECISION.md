## T05 independent custody and availability
Current intended successor uses five owners, threshold three. Normal quorum-authorized replacement is distinct from loss of quorum. Five distinct addresses alone cannot establish independent control.
- 0/1/2 unavailable owners: at least three remain; existing quorum may authorize permitted actions and reviewed rotation, subject to delay, validity, role exclusions, current epoch and exact signature policy. Not an instantaneous freeze or guaranteed recovery.
- 3/4/5 unavailable owners: fewer than three remain; ordinary quorum action and rotation unavailable. No hidden founder recovery, automatic threshold reduction, delegate/module or emergency bypass. Availability loss may be permanent. Three compromised owners are also sufficient to authorize allowed actions; policy caps reduce but do not eliminate abuse.
- Recommended baseline: independent owners with separately managed backups, succession arrangements and incident contact procedure; maintain exact threshold. Accept permanent below-quorum loss risk only with documented agreement before funding. If unacceptable, design a separately reviewed architecture before any deposit; do not improvise a recovery path on existing fixtures.
- Evidence for each owner: dedicated public test address, role separation, private record of independent operator/control and backup procedure, local challenge verification, conflicts and succession. Do not collect or publish seed phrases/private keys; signatures should not be placed in public issue. No production owner selected or verified.
