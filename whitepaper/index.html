01 / Executive summary

One connected ecosystem. Clear product boundaries.

Climboria is developing a browser-first game and supporting Web3 infrastructure. Its principal game destination is Jarrell’s Peak; the separate Arena is a future competitive destination. The wider ecosystem includes Climboria Signature DEX, Swap, Launchpad, a planned marketplace and CLIMBX, a personal trading assistant.

Products have separate delivery gates. A concept, interface preview or repository implementation must not be mistaken for a verified live service. Network transactions, token issuance, rewards and liquidity exist only when explicitly launched and independently verifiable.

Status principle: This whitepaper documents product intent and design requirements, not completed deployments or guaranteed timelines.

03 / Interactive world

Jarrell’s Peak is the foundation

The intended experience is an expansive wilderness without a summit or final level. Exploration and survival are the core loop, not speculative token activity. Arena is a separate future map for free-for-all fights, Guild Battles and funded tournaments; casual fights have no rewards.

Planned All-Time and Weekly Top Gainers leaderboards combine eligible $CLIMB earnings, treasure value and transparent rarity or difficulty bonuses. Any reward distribution requires verified funding, published eligibility and server-side anti-cheat. A proposed weekly settlement window is Friday 03:00 UTC, only if the system is launched and funded.

Future Global, Proximity and Private Chat require authentication, moderation, mute, block, report and anti-spam controls. Multiplayer capacity claims require load testing, not just design targets.

04 / Market infrastructure

Signature DEX and Swap

The DEX is intended to provide transparent Solana market access, asset information and trading interfaces. Swap is the transaction workflow. Production release requires reliable quotes, clear fee and slippage disclosures, route validation, wallet confirmation, transaction-state reconciliation, error handling and monitoring.

Market listings are not endorsements. Liquidity, prices and execution outcomes may change rapidly. Trading functionality must not be described as live until real network execution and operational checks are independently verified.

05 / Token infrastructure

Signature Launchpad

The proposed launchpad will support token creation and launch workflows. Production controls should cover verified transaction details, token authority disclosures, fee transparency, abuse monitoring and clear warnings that launching or listing a token does not establish legitimacy or investment quality.

Potential fee allocations to game development must be disclosed and accounted for separately from marketplace revenue. No launch, token address, liquidity commitment or token sale is guaranteed by this document.

06 / Automated trading

CLIMBX — You Dictate. It Climbs. It Learns.

Protect Capital First. Trade Second. Learn Continuously. CLIMBX is planned as a free-to-access personal trading assistant for Climboria Signature DEX only. Each wallet may configure one bot with up to five selected token assets. Strategy choices, asset limits and trade permissions belong to the wallet owner.

  1. Configure: select allowed assets, strategy, per-order and total spending limits, slippage tolerance and loss thresholds.
  2. Simulate first: paper-trading beta should display hypothetical fills, assumptions, fees, drawdowns and risk events without moving user funds.
  3. Review learning: the bot may propose strategy improvements based on completed trading history. Changes require explicit YES/NO approval and may never override hard risk limits.
  4. Consider live trading only after approval: future live orders must use owner-signed, scoped and expiring permissions and execute exclusively through Climboria Signature DEX.

Required safeguards include token allowlists, capped exposure, stop-loss and daily loss limits, emergency pause, owner revocation, replay protection, suspicious-token and liquidity checks, slippage and stale-price protection, audit logs and settlement reconciliation. These are requirements; not all are implemented or verified.

Current status: preview only; real-money execution disabled. First intended usable release is paper trading. Signed local-validator tests, devnet evidence, reviewed on-chain authorization, secure DEX-only routing and independent security assessment are required before any live beta.

Bot access is intended to be free. A proposed future live fee is 10% of eligible new cumulative net realized profit above a high-water mark, carrying forward prior losses; this is not an active or finalized fee. Network costs may apply. View CLIMBX preview.

08 / Technical architecture

Explicit boundaries between services

At the design level, the website and game clients interact with their respective backends; trading interfaces use Solana wallet authorization, market-data providers and transaction routing; marketplace and rewards require server-side eligibility and accounting. CLIMBX must use an isolated authorization and strategy layer, with its own state, risk policies and test coverage.

Security-sensitive systems should enforce authorization on the server or chain, not rely on hidden UI buttons. Signing, transaction submission, settlement verification, fee accounting and event logging need independent validation. The precise production architecture, contracts, custody model and operational service-level objectives remain subject to implementation and review.

09 / Trust and risk

Risk controls are release gates, not marketing claims

Wallet & contract safety

No seed-phrase collection; scoped approvals, expiration, revocation, signer verification, replay protection and independent review.

Execution safety

DEX-only route enforcement for CLIMBX, slippage and quote checks, spending caps, transaction reconciliation and failure handling.

Financial exposure

Loss limits, exposure limits, circuit breakers, suspicious-token screening and clear acknowledgement of residual risk.

Operational integrity

Access control, monitoring, incident response, audit trails, backup procedures and transparent user support.

Stop-losses can fail or execute at worse prices. Honeypots, rug pulls, MEV, outages, smart-contract bugs, liquidity gaps and poor strategies can cause partial or total loss. No system can guarantee profitable trades or eliminate all scams.

10 / Delivery plan

Release by verified capability

  1. Stabilize and verify existing Jarrell’s Peak gameplay and current website experiences.
  2. Complete Swap and DEX transaction paths with wallet, quote, settlement, monitoring and security tests.
  3. Complete identity and account experiences and progress toward a fully live game.
  4. Launchpad follows its own security, liquidity and operational readiness gates.
  5. Release CLIMBX paper trading with observable risk controls; evaluate limited live automation only after signed validator tests and audit.
  6. Expand marketplace, chat, Arena, sponsorship and funded reward features when their infrastructure, budget and safeguards are ready.

These are sequencing intentions, not fixed launch commitments. Each product requires a separate release decision.

11 / Ecosystem operations
