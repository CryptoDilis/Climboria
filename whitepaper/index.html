01. Executive overview

Climboria is a browser-first gaming and Web3 ecosystem centered on a physics-based climbing adventure and a wilderness survival experience. The project is designed to bring players, traders, creators, and builders into one connected brand and product environment.

The guiding principle is Game First, Speculation Later: build a compelling, accessible game experience first, then connect token and marketplace features through transparent, secure, independently testable integrations. The initial blockchain focus is Solana. Base is a later expansion target, subject to secure token representation, supply accounting, liquidity, and operational readiness.

Climboria's ecosystem is a phased product plan—not a claim that every described service is currently operational. Release status must be checked against official announcements and verifiable on-chain evidence.

03. Game: Jarrell's Peak

Jarrell's Peak is Climboria's special endless survival and exploration world. It is conceived as a large wilderness of forests, cliffs, rivers, caves, campsites, coastlines, landmarks, and hidden treasure routes. Fishing, swimming, resource discovery, and survival form part of the intended world loop.

The core risk/reward concept is that deeper exploration may reveal more valuable discoveries while increasing the risk of losing carried loot. Players may explore alone or cooperate in parties. Trust, competition, and betrayal are part of the intended high-risk social atmosphere.

Game scope and future systems

  • Jarrell's Peak is the special endless survival mode; it is not one of ten ordinary mountain levels.
  • Gameplay mechanics, death/respawn, loot persistence, combat, multiplayer, and economy behavior must be tested in the actual release before being represented as available.
  • A separate public Arena, guild battles, and Chatworld (global, proximity, and private messaging) are future systems and remain planned until released.
  • The core browser experience is intended to remain accessible to non-crypto players; wallet use should be required only for features that genuinely need on-chain interaction.

05. Climboria Swap

Swap is intended to provide a straightforward interface for exchanging supported tokens. The target flow is: select network and assets, connect a compatible wallet, request a real provider-backed quote, review execution details, sign in the wallet, submit the transaction, and confirm the on-chain result.

Before a swap is presented as successful, the system must reconcile the transaction outcome. The interface must handle unsupported assets, insufficient liquidity, stale quotes, rejected signatures, expired transactions, network errors, and uncertain transaction states.

Release gate: production backend and provider integration, accurate quote and fee details, wallet review/sign/submit/confirm/reconcile, security review, monitoring, and end-to-end testing. Mock quotes or simulated fills must never be presented as real trades.

06. Climboria DEX

The DEX is planned as a broader market-discovery experience, distinct from the focused Swap flow. Planned capabilities include token search and profiles, watchlists, exact network and mint identity, provider-backed prices and liquidity, pool information, charts, market history where available, and access to supported swap routes.

Market data must be attributable to reliable providers and clearly contextualized. The product must not fabricate prices, candles, volume, liquidity, pool depth, or trading activity. Stale, unavailable, and error states must be explicit.

08. Marketplace, crafting, and digital ownership

The planned marketplace is intended for eligible ingredients, crafted gear, and digital items, with $CLIMB as the intended marketplace currency. Players should be able to use eligible items in-game without minting. Where NFT minting is supported, it is intended to be optional; players pay applicable blockchain network gas fees.

Documented fee design

  • Proposed marketplace fee: 2.5% on buys and 2.5% on sells.
  • Five percent of actually collected marketplace fee revenue is intended for the Climboria game funding pool. This is not 5% of gross trading volume.
  • These are design rules, not evidence of a live fee contract or active distribution.

Forge and refinement systems are intended to let players use collected materials to create or improve equipment. Recipes, item attributes, rarity, crafting costs, tradability, ownership, and settlement rules must be disclosed and tested before activation.

10. Multi-chain architecture and Base strategy

The project follows a phased chain strategy rather than claiming simultaneous multi-chain availability.

  1. Phase 1 — Solana: initial $CLIMB token focus and initial ecosystem integrations.
  2. Phase 2 — Base: planned 1:1 $CLIMB deployment or representation after canonical token identity, supply accounting, cross-chain security, wallet support, and liquidity are verified.
  3. Phase 3 — Base Swap/DEX: support for eligible Base assets and $CLIMB through real market-data and swap/liquidity providers.
  4. Future networks: considered only where user demand, secure infrastructure, and sustainable liquidity justify expansion.

Before Base is described as live, Climboria must publish canonical token addresses on both networks, explain the exact issuance/bridge/representation model, demonstrate safeguards against double representation, and verify the cross-chain mechanism. A 1:1 token-unit design does not guarantee equal market prices. No bridge or cross-chain transfer should be advertised before implementation and independent review.

12. Security, transparency, and risk disclosures

  • Market risk: crypto assets are volatile and users may lose some or all of the value they commit. No return or profit is promised.
  • Smart-contract risk: contracts, wallets, bridges, liquidity providers, and external services may contain vulnerabilities or fail.
  • Execution risk: prices, liquidity, slippage, fees, transaction confirmation, and availability can vary by network and provider.
  • Token identity risk: users must verify the correct network and canonical token address through official channels and on-chain records.
  • Game and reward risk: game items, rewards, and marketplace features depend on implementation, funding, eligibility, and published rules.
  • Operational risk: third-party providers, network congestion, outages, and security incidents may interrupt services.
  • Regulatory risk: laws and platform requirements differ by jurisdiction and may change; users are responsible for understanding applicable restrictions.

Users should independently assess risks, verify every transaction, and never rely on unofficial token links or impersonator accounts. Climboria will label roadmap items accurately and publish material changes through official channels.

15. Player experience, progression, and fair free-to-play design

Climboria is intended to be free-to-play. The core adventure should be playable without purchases or a crypto wallet. Optional Web3 functions must not become a paywall for basic access to the game.

Starting character and equipment

  • Players are intended to choose a free default male or female adult hero, designed around a mid-30s age style.
  • Every player begins with one free starter climbing tool. The starter tool is intended to be non-sellable, non-transferable, and non-mintable.
  • The free hero and starter tool are intended to provide a complete no-purchase route through the core game.
  • Additional character styles, age options, tools, and visual skins may be offered later through crafting or the marketplace. Any gameplay attributes must be visible, balanced, and published before release.
  • No purchasable item should be required to complete the core game. Attribute bonuses and expedition scoring must be balanced so spending alone does not decide competition.

Collect, Forge, and refine

Players are intended to collect ingredients through gameplay and use them in the Forge to craft or improve tools. Refining is designed to have no platform fee, although ingredients may be consumed. Recipes, ingredient requirements, success rules, item statistics, rarity, and any failure or loss conditions must be disclosed before the feature goes live.

Game menu and supporting panels

The current design describes four menu entries: Jarrell’s Peak (the playable launch-world destination), Arena (a future-feature status panel), Marketplace (a demo storefront), and Inventory (a local preview). Only Jarrell’s Peak and Arena are game-world destinations; Marketplace and Inventory are supporting panels. A preview or local inventory is not proof of persistent ownership or live trading.

16. Treasure chests, Top Gainers, and reward controls

Random discovery chests

The design includes randomly placed treasure chests that may contain $CLIMB, ingredients, gear, or eligible digital items. Chest coordinates are intended to remain undisclosed until a player discovers a chest; discovery may then be announced and recorded with the disclosed location and contents. The current design has no daily or weekly claim cap, but this must not be interpreted as an active unlimited payout promise.

Before any chest reward is activated, Climboria must publish the reward odds or deterministic content rules, funding source, eligibility, anti-cheat controls, duplicate/account-abuse handling, and the method used to verify discoveries and distributions. Rewards must be funded and secured before they are advertised.

All-Time and Weekly Top Gainers

  • A permanent All-Time Top Gainers board and a separate Weekly Top Gainers board are planned for Jarrell’s Peak.
  • Scoring is intended to combine eligible $CLIMB earnings, eligible treasure value, and transparent rarity/difficulty bonuses.
  • Weekly payout timing in the current design is Friday at 03:00 UTC (11:00 AM Philippine time), only if the reward program is funded and activated.
  • There is no summit ranking or badge system in the current design direction.
  • CLIMB Points, campaign progress, and leaderboard scores are not themselves tokens and do not guarantee a payout.

Reward integrity

Any reward program requires published eligibility, calculation windows, funding proof, server-side anti-cheat, wallet/account controls, payout reconciliation, dispute procedures, and clear notice of suspension or cancellation conditions. No reward is guaranteed without a verified funded pool.

17. Future Arena, Guild Battles, and competitive events

Arena is planned as a separate public forest battle and event map, not a second version of Jarrell’s Peak. The intended modes include free-for-all combat, Guild Battles, and sponsored tournaments. Casual fights are intended to remain free and carry no prize rewards.

Guild Battle rules in the current design

  • During a Guild Battle, the whole Arena becomes the battlefield for the two participating guilds; other players spectate.
  • A guild member may request a battle, while guild leadership approval is required before it is scheduled or activated.
  • Weekly Guild Wars depend on budget, secure multiplayer infrastructure, anti-cheat, published scoring, and tested event operations.
  • Badges are deferred and are not part of the current launch design.

Scale and technical gate

A single shared-world target above 1,000 concurrent players has been discussed as a design ambition only. It is not a current capacity claim. Server architecture, synchronization, moderation, abuse controls, and load testing must demonstrate the actual safe capacity before any number is advertised.

Optional wager matches

Optional wager matches have been discussed as a future feature using mutually accepted $CLIMB stakes. The proposed platform fee is 2.5% of the total pot, with the fee intended for the game funding pool. This must not be activated without secure escrow, explicit consent, deterministic settlement, dispute handling, anti-collusion/abuse controls, and legal/compliance review. Casual matches remain separate and free.

Climboria Signature is the project's responsible-launch philosophy.   • Climboria administration retains final approval. Sponsorship requires an eligibility review and a written agreement describing package, fees, prize pool, duration, exclusivity, placements, cancellation/refund terms, postponement, force majeure, renewal, and prize protection.
  • The full agreed sponsorship fee and the complete promised prize pool must be received and verified before any public announcement, sponsor branding, or event activation.

Jarrell’s Peak funding boundary

A disclosed portion of eligible future DEX and Launchpad fees is intended to support Jarrell’s Peak directly, but exact percentages and accounting rules are not finalized. 19. Chatworld, guilds, and Social Lobby

Guilds, referrals, and community campaigns

Guilds are intended to let players create or join teams, coordinate limited-time events, complete shared challenges, and participate in competitions. Referrals, community campaigns, and a campaign/reward tracker may show participation, challenge progress, and eligibility. Communication and moderation

Future Chatworld features include Global Chat, Proximity Chat, and Private Messages for Jarrell’s Peak and the future Arena. The design permits ordinary competitive banter within conduct rules, while threats, targeted harassment, hate speech, scams, and spam may be moderated. Required controls include mute, block, report, anti-spam, moderator tooling, server-side identity, permissions, message delivery, and persistence.

Browser access and future mobile app

The core adventure is intended to run in desktop and mobile browsers, with touch controls treated as a first-class experience. A dedicated Climboria mobile app is a future possibility, not a released product or a dated commitment. The priority is to stabilize browser gameplay, touch controls, performance, and account experience before preparing and testing an app release. The core game should remain accessible to non-crypto players without requiring a wallet.

Social Lobby and trading hangout

A shared Social Lobby is planned as a place to meet, chat, display character skins and tools, and find other players between expeditions. A future in-lobby trading terminal may combine token search and watchlists with an order book and candlestick chart. This depends on reliable market data, wallet integration, order handling, security, and moderation; it is not a live trading claim.

20. Item ownership, NFT eligibility, and wallet boundaries

The design direction is for items earned or obtained through gameplay to be eligible for optional NFT minting where the item type, ownership model, game economy, and technical implementation support it. Items should remain usable in-game without minting. An item becomes externally tradable only after it is minted and supported by the relevant marketplace and network; minting does not guarantee liquidity or a buyer. Climboria intends no platform minting fee, while the player pays network gas. The free starter tool is excluded and is intended to remain non-sellable, non-transferable, and non-mintable.

Ownership, metadata, burn/upgrade behavior, anti-duplication, custody, and transfer rules must be implemented and published before activation. Wallet connection should be required only for features that genuinely need an on-chain action, such as supported swaps, token settlement, or NFT operations. A connected wallet is not proof of identity for every off-chain feature; the product must explain which account or wallet controls each asset and action.
