2. Game — Jarrell’s Peak

Jarrell’s Peak is a browser-first climbing, survival, and open-world treasure-hunting experience. Players explore rivers, forests, cliffs, caves, camps, coastlines, landmarks, and hidden routes. Fishing, swimming, resource discovery, and survival form the intended world loop.

The core risk/reward loop: venture farther into dangerous territory for potentially better discoveries, while accepting greater risk of losing carried loot. Players may cooperate or compete over discoveries. A separate Arena, guild battles, and Chatworld are future work until implemented and verified.

3. $CLIMB token strategy

$CLIMB is the intended ticker, with Solana as the initial chain focus. The project’s planned launch target is 15 November 2026 at 15:00 UTC. This is a target announcement, not proof of a completed launch. Publish the mint address, supply, authorities, distribution, and live trading status only after verification through official Climboria channels and on-chain evidence.

Token utility, eligibility, fees, and any rewards must be disclosed accurately. This document does not invent a mint address, final supply, allocation, or liquidity commitment.

4. Climboria Swap

Swap is intended to provide a focused exchange flow for supported assets. Users select a network and token pair, enter an amount, receive a real provider-backed quote, review rate/route/price impact/slippage/network fee/expiry, then approve and sign in their wallet. The service must submit, confirm, and reconcile the transaction outcome.

It must handle stale quotes, insufficient liquidity, rejected signatures, expired transactions, network errors, and uncertain outcomes honestly. No fake quotes, simulated fills, or placeholder success may be shown as real.

Not production-live until the full real transaction lifecycle is verified

5. Climboria DEX

The DEX is the broader trading and market-discovery experience, distinct from the focused Swap flow. Planned features include search and discovery, watchlists, token profiles, exact mint/network identity, provider-backed price/liquidity/volume/pool data, charts, market history, and access to supported swap routes.

Market data must come from real attributable providers. Prices, candles, liquidity, volume, and trading activity must never be fabricated. Clear stale-data, unavailable, loading, and error states are required.

Not production-live until provider data and trading flows are verified

6. Climboria Launchpad

The planned Launchpad will let projects apply to launch with structured disclosures and review. Intended stages include team and official-link verification, token configuration and authority review, tokenomics and distribution checks, published sale terms, eligibility and wallet limits, anti-bot controls, verified wallet/on-chain execution, and post-launch disclosure.

Sale terms must disclose supply, price or pricing method, sale dates, allocations, vesting/lockups, fees, use of proceeds, liquidity plan, and cancellation/refund conditions. A Launchpad listing is not a guarantee of safety, legitimacy, liquidity, exchange listing, or profit.

Later delivery priority · not open for live launches

7. Marketplace, crafting & digital ownership

The planned marketplace is intended for eligible ingredients, crafted gear, and digital items, using $CLIMB as the intended currency. Players may use eligible items in-game without minting. NFT minting is intended to be optional; players pay applicable network gas fees.

The documented design proposes 2.5% buy and 2.5% sell marketplace fees. Five percent of actually collected marketplace fee revenue is intended for the Climboria game funding pool—not 5% of trading volume. These are design rules, not evidence of live fee collection or rewards. Forge and refinement systems remain subject to implementation and testing.

8. Community, rewards & social features

Planned features include player profiles, referrals, events, guilds, All-Time and weekly Top Gainers boards, and funded reward campaigns. Rewards must be funded, secured, and governed by published eligibility and calculation rules before activation.

Future Global, Proximity, and Private Chat, parties, and Social Lobby features require server-authoritative identity, permissions, persistence, moderation, reporting, mute/block, and anti-spam controls. Arena wagers and sponsored events require secure escrow or verified funding, written agreements, and legal/compliance review before activation.

9. Solana first — planned Base expansion

Phase 1 · Solana

Initial $CLIMB token focus and first ecosystem integrations.

Phase 2 · Base

Planned 1:1 $CLIMB deployment/representation after canonical token identity, supply accounting, cross-chain security, wallet support, and liquidity are verified.

Phase 3 · Base Swap/DEX

Planned Base market access for supported Base assets and $CLIMB using real market data and swap/liquidity providers.

Future networks

Considered only when demand, secure infrastructure, and sustainable liquidity justify expansion.

A 1:1 token-unit design does not guarantee equal market prices across chains. The precise bridge or token-representation model, canonical addresses, and controls against double representation must be published and independently reviewed before cross-chain transfers are advertised as available.
