The wider Climboria ecosystem

Built in stages, not all at once.

Marketplace & NFT minting

Every item obtained or earned through gameplay is intended to be eligible for NFT minting. Items can be used in-game without minting; once an item is minted as an NFT, it can be traded or sold through supported marketplaces, subject to ownership, item, and network rules. Climboria plans no platform minting fee, while the player pays the blockchain gas fee. This system is planned and is not live yet.

PLANNED · PLAYER PAYS GAS

Climboria DEX · normal crypto trading

The goal is a familiar decentralized exchange experience: connect a supported wallet, choose a token pair, review the quoted rate, price impact, slippage settings, and network fee, then confirm the swap yourself. The interface is planned to show available liquidity, price charts, and transaction history where reliable data is available. Trades depend on liquidity and can lose value; prices, execution, and fills are not guaranteed. The DEX is planned, not live.

PLANNED · SWAP-BASED TRADING

Multi-chain vision

Climboria aims to expand beyond a single blockchain over time. Solana is the initial focus, Base is a later consideration, and additional networks may follow as secure cross-chain infrastructure, supported assets, and liquidity become available. “Multi-chain” does not mean every chain is supported at launch.

Climboria Launchpad

Project launches with transparent terms — planned, not live.

Apply & verify

Projects will submit their team and official links, token configuration, tokenomics, intended utility, distribution plan, and liquidity approach. Climboria plans a review process for identity and project disclosures, authority controls, and available security evidence. Review is not a guarantee of safety, legitimacy, or profit.

PROPOSED REVIEW RULES

Publish sale terms first

Before participation opens, each project must publish its supply, price or pricing method, sale dates, allocation limits, vesting or lockups, fees, use of proceeds, liquidity plan, and refund/cancellation conditions. Any changes must be disclosed before users commit funds.

TRANSPARENT TERMS

Fair access & launch disclosure

Each sale will publish eligibility, wallet limits, allocation and oversubscription rules, anti-bot/anti-sybil measures, and any region restrictions. After a launch, the project should disclose the verified token address and relevant sale/liquidity results. A Launchpad listing does not promise a listing elsewhere, liquidity, price appreciation, or profit.

RULES BEFORE EACH SALE
Launchpad principles: no project is automatically approved; no guaranteed allocation or return; disclose team/project risks, token distribution, unlock schedules, fees, liquidity arrangements, and conflicts of interest. Launches only proceed after required technical, security, operational, and legal/compliance checks are ready. Exact platform fees and launch formats will be published before the first sale.

PROJECT BRIEF · GAME FIRST

What Climboria is building

Climboria is a browser-first climbing, survival, and open-world treasure-hunting project. The initial world focus is Jarrell’s Peak, an endless-feeling wilderness with no summit or final level. The guiding principle is Game First, Speculation Later; the core adventure is intended to remain accessible without a crypto wallet or required purchases.

Jarrell’s Peak

Explore, survive, fish, swim, and discover hidden treasure across a vast wilderness. There is no summit, final level, or safe zone. The ten previously proposed normal mountains are deferred and are not launch content.

Top Gainers

A permanent All-Time board and a separate Weekly Top Gainers board are planned. Scoring combines eligible $CLIMB earnings, eligible treasure value, and transparent rarity/difficulty bonuses. No summit leaderboard or badge system is planned.

Marketplace & game funding

Planned marketplace buy and sell fees are 2.5% each. Five percent of actual collected marketplace fee revenue is allocated to the Climboria game funding pool—not 5% of trading volume. Eligible items and crafted gear are intended to use $CLIMB only. Systems are not live until implemented and verified.

Future Arena, Chatworld & sponsors

The public Arena, Global/Proximity/Private Chat, optional wagers, sponsor events, and Weekly Guild Wars are future features. Sponsorship requires admin approval, a written agreement, and full verified payment—including the complete prize pool—before any announcement or activation.
