10 expedition mountains

The planned main game features 10 long, challenging mountain expeditions. Players start from a safe base, manage health, stamina, weather, gear durability, supplies, and risk while climbing. At zero health, a player returns to the mountain’s starting safe zone; the precise loot penalty is part of the game rules to be finalized.

EXPLORATION REWARD CONCEPT

Jarrell’s Peak: endless risk

Jarrell’s Peak is envisioned as an endless, unmapped survival mountain with no safe zone, black market, or forge. Difficulty and danger rise as players advance. Loot bags may remain at a death site for a limited time and can be looted by others before they decay; rescue mechanics are planned. Optional SOL donations may fund its treasure, with conversion to $CLIMB only through a verified process before distribution.

Every item obtained or earned through gameplay is intended to be eligible for NFT minting. Items can be used in-game without minting; once an item is minted as an NFT, it can be traded or sold through supported marketplaces, subject to ownership, item, and network rules. The goal is a familiar decentralized exchange experience: connect a supported wallet, choose a token pair, review the quoted rate, price impact, slippage settings, and network fee, then confirm the swap yourself. Trades depend on liquidity and can lose value; prices, execution, and fills are not guaranteed. The DEX is planned, not live.

PROJECT BRIEF · GAME FIRST

What Climboria is building

Climboria is a browser-first climbing game built around skill, physical movement, progression, competition, and community. The guiding principle is simple: make the game worth playing before asking players to care about tokens or trading. The core experience is intended to remain accessible without a crypto wallet or required purchases.

Gameplay & progression

Players climb challenging mountain levels, improve their technique, collect eligible materials, and work toward tools and crafting through the planned Forge. A free starter route is intended to remain viable; optional purchases must not be required to finish the game.

Expedition records & recognition

Each normal mountain is intended to keep summit-run records with player display name, completion time, retries, and points. A funded weekly pool is planned to pay eligible players proportionally to their points. Permanent non-transferable emblems may recognize achievements.

Digital items & marketplace

Eligible items may later be minted as NFTs and traded through supported marketplaces. In-game use should not require minting. Minting requires network gas; marketplace fees, eligibility, ownership rules, and any buy/sell tax must be disclosed before trading opens.

Token & security status

Solana is the initial blockchain focus; Base is a later possibility. $CLIMB is a candidate ticker, not proof of an official token. Verify the contract address only through Climboria’s official channels. Swap, Launchpad, DEX, rewards, and other planned features must not be treated as live until released.
