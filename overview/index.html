OFFICIAL PROJECT OVERVIEW

One climb.
Room to grow.

Climboria is building a browser-based climbing game focused on skill, progression, crafting, and community, with a dedicated mobile app planned for the future. The game comes first; the app, competitive systems, and Web3 features will be introduced in stages as they are built, tested, and ready.

The game comes first

Game first. Speculation later.

⛰️

Browser climbing game

A climbing experience designed for web browsers, with mobile and desktop play in mind. The aim is to make the core game fun and accessible without requiring crypto.

CORE EXPERIENCE
🧗

Levels and progression

Progress through challenging climbs, learn the mechanics, improve your runs, and work toward more mountain themes and level content over time.

EXPANDING
📱

Dedicated mobile app

A Climboria app is planned for the future. The priority is to polish gameplay and touch controls first, then prepare and test a mobile release before announcing platform availability.

FUTURE PLAN · NOT RELEASED
🏆

Seasons and rankings

Seasonal competition is planned, including leaderboards and a Top 100 ranking concept. Season rules, scoring, and eligibility must be published before rewards go live.

PLANNED

Forge, refine, and collect

Progression beyond the climb.

⚒️

Forge and refine

Gather or earn materials and ingredients, then use a future Forge system to craft, upgrade, or refine climbing tools and equipment. Recipes, costs, and success rules will be defined in-game before launch.

PLANNED SYSTEM
🧰

Tools, skins, and inventory

Future player profiles and inventories may track tools, equipment, ingredients, character skins, and progression records, giving players ways to personalize their climb.

PLANNED SYSTEM
🎖️

Badges and achievements

Badges are envisioned as achievement and season recognition items, with rarity tiers and special recognition for top-ranked players. Some badges may later support ecosystem voting, with clear rules and no governance rights implied until implemented.

PLANNED SYSTEM

Community and rewards

Participation should be clear and fair.

⭐

CLIMB Points and streaks

A planned progression layer may include CLIMB Points, daily streaks, and challenges to recognize activity. Points are not a promise of token value, cash, or an airdrop.

PLANNED
🤝

Referrals and events

Referral features and community events are planned to help players invite friends and compete together. Eligibility and anti-abuse rules should be shared before any rewards are distributed.

PLANNED
🎁

Reward and airdrop tracker

A future tracker may show event participation or reward eligibility. No airdrop, allocation, or payout is guaranteed unless officially announced with published terms.

NOT A GUARANTEE

The wider Climboria ecosystem

Built in stages, not all at once.

🛒

Marketplace

A future marketplace may support eligible tools, ingredients, skins, and badges. Tradability, ownership, fees, and supported networks will depend on implementation and published rules.

FUTURE
🔁

Temporary swap → Climboria DEX

The current swap concept is only a temporary stepping stone. The long-term vision is to build Climboria DEX as our own decentralized exchange, subject to engineering, liquidity, security reviews, and a separate launch announcement.

FUTURE VISION · NOT LIVE
🚀

Launchpad and Web3

A launchpad and on-chain game records are longer-term ideas, not live services. The intended chain sequence is to evaluate Solana first and consider Base later when the game and infrastructure are ready.

FUTURE VISION

Development roadmap

Priorities may change as testing reveals what needs attention.

01

Stabilize the core game

Prioritize reliable gameplay, controls, camera behavior, readable mobile layouts, and a smooth browser experience.

02

Expand levels and seasons

Improve level selection and progression, then develop season rules, leaderboard scoring, and competitive events.

03

Build crafting and player systems

Design the Forge and refine loops, inventory, tools, ingredients, skins, badges, profiles, and achievement records.

04

Connect community features

Develop CLIMB Points, daily streaks, referrals, event tracking, and transparent reward eligibility rules.

05

Evaluate marketplace and Web3

Validate ownership and wallet integrations, consider a marketplace and on-chain records, and evaluate Solana first with Base as a later possibility.

06

Prepare the Climboria mobile app

Polish the browser game, mobile controls, and core levels first, then package and test an app release. Store submission and availability will be announced only when ready.

07

Work toward Climboria DEX

Move beyond the temporary swap concept toward a dedicated Climboria DEX only after architecture, liquidity, security, and operational requirements are met.
