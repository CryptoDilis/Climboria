CLIMBX is being designed as one free personal bot per wallet, supporting up to five selected assets. You choose the strategy, limits and whether to accept learning recommendations. Future orders must execute exclusively through Climboria Signature DEX, not external DEXs.

Paper trading comes first. The first intended usable release will simulate trades without moving funds, clearly distinguishing hypothetical results from real trading. The current page is an informational preview, not an active paper-trading service.

Learning stays under your control. The planned bot may propose changes based on trading history, but it must not change approved strategies or expand permissions without your YES/NO approval.

Security and capital protection · Planned requirements

  • Owner-signed, time-limited permissions; no seed phrase or private-key collection
  • Per-order and cumulative spending caps, token allowlist, revocation and replay protection
  • Stop-loss, daily loss limits, maximum exposure and automatic pause controls
  • Token screening, slippage and quote checks, suspicious-liquidity safeguards
  • Clear transaction history, risk warnings and user-approved strategy updates

Not all safeguards are implemented or independently verified. Real-money execution remains disabled pending signed Solana validator tests, secure DEX-only routing, reconciliation and independent security review. A stop-loss or token screening cannot guarantee against losses or scams.

Risks and release stages

Crypto trading can cause partial or total loss. Slippage, rug pulls, honeypots, outages, MEV, contract bugs and strategy errors remain possible. Simulated performance does not predict live returns. No guaranteed profits.

Now: preview only, no wallet or trades. Next intended stage: clearly labeled paper-trading beta. Future possibility: limited live beta only after security testing, audit and explicit approval.
